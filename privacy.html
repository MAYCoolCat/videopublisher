Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VideoPublisher handles user information.

2. Information collected

VideoPublisher is a desktop application. Depending on the features used, it may process local videos, images, captions, publishing schedules and authentication credentials.

3. Storage

Application data and authentication files may be stored locally on the user's computer.

4. Third-party services

When users connect YouTube or TikTok, the application sends the information necessary for requested operations through official platform APIs.

5. Data sharing

VideoPublisher does not sell users' personal information.

6. Security

Users should protect their computers, access tokens and authentication credentials. Access can be revoked through the respective platform settings.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.
